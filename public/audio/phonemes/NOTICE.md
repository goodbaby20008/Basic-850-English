# Phoneme audio notice

The 48 short MP3 clips in this directory are reproducible, synthetic teaching
samples generated locally with eSpeak NG 1.52.0 and its `en-gb` voice data.
They are broad reference targets for the phoneme cards, not recordings of a
specific speaker and not a claim that every English accent realizes a phoneme
in exactly the same way.

Generation mapping and settings are kept in
`scripts/build-phoneme-audio.ps1`. The eSpeak NG project is available at
https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng and is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
